Before you can install Haliade, you must get the HaliadeSYS ISO first.

The ISO is around 1.4 gigabytes, or slightly more depending on what your
system says. You can get it in one of two ways:

-- Via the Haliade website (if it is up)
-- Via the project's github page

Download it, and you are ready for the next step.

Obviously and sadly so, Haliade does not work on every machine, and does not
support every architecture. To boot Haliade, your machine should:

-- Have UEFI support (as BIOS support has not been implemented)
-- Have an X86_64 format
-- Have at least 2 gigabytes of RAM

And you should be good to go.

If your machine does not meet these requirements, Haliade will not boot for
you, so please check before you download and flash anything.
